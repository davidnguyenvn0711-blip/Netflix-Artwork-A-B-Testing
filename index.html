A few takeaways

Good decisions start with a better question.

The numbers are useful, but the real value is knowing what to do with them.

01

Give each version a fair chance

Viewers were split between the two images, so the comparison stayed balanced.

02

Look beyond the winning number

A higher click rate is promising. A real launch should also check watch time and whether people keep enjoying the show.

03

Test, learn, then decide

One result is a useful signal. Repeating the test with real viewers would make the decision stronger.

My recommendation

Try the stronger artwork, then keep listening.

Artwork B is the better choice in this simulation. Before making a real change, I would confirm the result with actual viewers and track what happens after the click.

Show how the test worked

1,000 simulated users were randomly assigned to artwork A or B. Each person's click was recorded, then the click rates were compared using a two proportion statistical test.

  • Artwork A: 117 clicks out of 492 viewers, or 23.8%.
  • Artwork B: 154 clicks out of 508 viewers, or 30.1%.
  • The difference was statistically significant in this simulation (p = 0.004).
  • The run used a fixed random seed so the example can be reproduced.

The dataset is simulated. It illustrates an analysis approach and does not use Netflix viewer data.
